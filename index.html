My Unofficial Local Guide to Bleach

  • Zanpakuto

    Zanpakuto is the form that a shinigami, or soul reaper first gets their weapon in. It is a sword that is unique to each shinigami and has its own personality. The zanpakuto can be used in its sealed form, but it can also be released into its shikai and bankai forms, which are more powerful.



  • Shikai

    A characters shikai is the first awakaned form of their zanpakuto, when they finally are in contact with their spirit. A shikai is a powerful ability that allows the user to control their zanpakuto in a more advanced way.



  • Bankai

    Bankai is the most powerful form of a zanpakuto. It the outermost projection of the user's spiritual power and being intune with oneself. For some, it is a an amplification of their shikai, and for others it is a completely new set of skills, technique, or abilities.
